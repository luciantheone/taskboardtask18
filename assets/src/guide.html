DO I NEED KYC?

KYC is needed for anything you sign on Redbelly mainnet

Chain ID 151. Anywhere else, no Redbelly KYC.
Hold RBNT on an exchange
NOT NEEDED
Nothing is signed on Redbelly Network.
Hold wrapped RBNT on another chain
NOT NEEDED
The token lives on another chain.
Bridge into Redbelly mainnet
UNCONFIRMED
No official page says. Enable your wallet first.
Bridge out of Redbelly mainnet
NEEDED
You sign on Redbelly Network.
Send, swap or use apps on mainnet
NEEDED
Write access needs an enabled account.
Stake RBNT
NEEDED
Staking is a mainnet transaction.
Native vs wrapped. Signing on Redbelly (chain ID 151) needs KYC. Wrapped RBNT on another chain does not.
DAO vs network. The DAO is open to general members from any region. High Council and leadership limits remain. The network does not offer services in 18 jurisdictions (terms, clause 15.1).
